BUSINESS COACHING FAQ

Questions, Answered

Who is Business Coaching with Kriishna Singhal for?

It is designed for business owners who want stronger leadership, more reliable sales processes, better systems and less owner dependency.

What is the C.L.O.S.E. Methodology?

C.L.O.S.E. is the working framework used across the coaching journey: Calibrate, Layer, Optimize, Standardize and Expand.

Will the coaching focus only on sales?

No. The framework connects leadership, sales, team behavior, process architecture and systems so growth is not dependent on one person.

What does AI-assisted optimization mean?

It means identifying repeatable workflows where appropriate AI assistance can improve speed and consistency while keeping the underlying business process clear and controlled.

Is this a one-size-fits-all program?

No. Your business stage, current challenges and goals are used to focus the coaching conversation on the areas that matter most to your situation.

What happens after I submit the application?

Your responses are reviewed before the strategy conversation so the next step can be aligned with your business context and goals.

01

ADDIE Leadership Development Model

Analysis. Design. Develop. Implementation. Evolution.

Leadership capability is built through a structured learning cycle. We use the ADDIE model to move from understanding the real leadership gap to designing practical interventions, applying them in the workplace and reviewing what changes.

A
Analysis — Understand the leadership gap Identify the current team context, leadership challenge, stakeholder expectations, behaviour patterns and the capability that needs to improve.
D
Design — Define the learning path Set clear leadership outcomes and decide which conversations, behaviours, scenarios and practice activities will help the learner move toward those outcomes.
D
Develop — Build practical leadership tools Create role-play situations, reflection prompts, decision frameworks, delegation exercises, feedback routines and action plans around the actual leadership environment.
I
Implementation — Apply learning in real situations Put the learning into practice through team meetings, difficult conversations, delegation, accountability and day-to-day leadership decisions.
E
Evolution — Review, learn and improve Review what changed, gather feedback, identify what still needs attention and continuously refine the leadership approach for the next cycle.
ADDIE
Why this model matters

Leadership development should connect learning with behaviour.

ADDIE gives the leadership journey a clear structure: understand the problem, design the right learning experience, build practical tools, apply them in context and evolve the approach based on what changes. That keeps leadership development connected to real team situations rather than treating learning as a one-time session.

In practice Communication, delegation, accountability, decision-making, team culture and people leadership can each become focused development themes inside the cycle.
